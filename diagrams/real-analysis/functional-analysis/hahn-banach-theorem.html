Hahn–Banach theorem

A linear measurement known only on a subspace can be extended to the whole space without violating a prescribed sublinear bound. The same principle creates separating hyperplanes, norming functionals, and supporting lines of convex sets.

Incorrect: the algebraic theorem needs completeness.

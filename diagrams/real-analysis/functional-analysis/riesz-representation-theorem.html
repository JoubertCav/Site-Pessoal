Hilbert-space duality

Every continuous measurement has a direction.

A continuous linear functional on a Hilbert space is not an abstract external object: it is inner product with one unique vector. The theorem turns duality into geometry.

1 · The mathematical question

Can the dual space be seen inside the original space?

For a general Banach space, a functional need not be represented by a vector of the same space. The inner product gives Hilbert spaces an exceptional self-duality.

Input

A continuous functional

It assigns one scalar to each vector, linearly and continuously.

Hidden geometry

A unique direction

The vector is normal to the kernel and records both the direction and strength of the functional.

Identification

Functional equals inner product

All values of the functional are recovered by measuring components along one vector.

9 · What can go wrong?

Remove a hypothesis and the identification breaks

The theorem is not a generic fact about all normed spaces or all algebraic functionals.

Banach duality need not be self-duality

For , the dual is , not naturally . There is no inner product whose induced norm is the -norm.

Failed conclusion. One cannot represent every functional by inner product with a vector of the original Banach space. Repair: require Hilbert geometry.

Algebraic functionals may be unbounded

On an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space, Hamel-basis constructions produce linear functionals that are not continuous.

Proof break. The kernel need not be closed, so the projection theorem cannot be applied. No vector can represent such a functional, because inner-product functionals are automatically bounded.

Evaluation is not an L² functional

The expression is not even well-defined on equivalence classes, since changing a function at one point does not change its class.

Failed object. There is no satisfying for all . Repair: use a reproducing-kernel Hilbert space where evaluation is continuous.

Complex linearity depends on convention

With inner product linear in the first variable, is linear in , but the map is conjugate-linear.

Common source of sign and conjugation errors. State which slot is linear before writing the theorem or its coordinate formula.

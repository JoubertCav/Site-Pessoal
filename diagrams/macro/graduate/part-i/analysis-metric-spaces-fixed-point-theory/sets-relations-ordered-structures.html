Existence principle

Zorn's Lemma

A chain is a totally ordered subset. A maximal element has no strictly larger feasible element; it need not dominate every element of the set.

Zorn's lemma is equivalent to the axiom of choice. In analysis and economics, it enters indirectly through Hahn–Banach and the separation arguments behind welfare theorems.

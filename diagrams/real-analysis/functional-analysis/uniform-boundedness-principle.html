Final synthesis

The theorem works by converting infinitely many vector-dependent estimates into a countable closed cover. Baire forces one piece of that cover to contain a ball. Linearity moves the ball to the origin; homogeneity expands the local estimate to all scales. The apparent quantifier reversal is therefore a geometric consequence of completeness.

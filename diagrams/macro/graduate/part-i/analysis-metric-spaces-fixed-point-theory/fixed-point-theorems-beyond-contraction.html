Fixed-point theorems beyond contraction

For economists, a fixed point is a self-consistent object: a price vector that reproduces itself under market adjustment, a value or policy function that is unchanged by an operator, or a strategy profile that is a best reply to itself. This revised lab makes the geometry explicit: what each theorem assumes, what it visually means, and what kind of economic argument it supports.

Common mistakes

Terms going to zero is not enough.The harmonic series is the standard counterexample.
Positive series are monotone in partial sums.Then boundedness is the key issue.
Absolute convergence is stronger.Conditional convergence is a separate phenomenon.
Ratio and root tests are silent at 1.Do not force a conclusion there.
Alternating test needs monotone decay.Cancellation alone is not the theorem.
Condensation has hypotheses.It applies to nonnegative nonincreasing terms.

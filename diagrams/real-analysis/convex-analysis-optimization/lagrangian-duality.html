3 · Weak duality

Every feasible multiplier gives a lower bound

Weak duality is purely algebraic and does not require convexity.

Taking the infimum over \(x\), then the infimum over primal feasible points, gives \(q(\lambda,\nu)\le p^\star\).

Why λ≥0?

For a feasible inequality \(f_i(x)\le0\), multiplying by \(\lambda_i\ge0\) preserves \(\lambda_i f_i(x)\le0\). A negative multiplier would reverse the sign and destroy the lower-bound guarantee.

12 · What can go wrong?

Weak duality is universal; strong duality is not

Each failure mode removes a different part of the optimality certificate.

A negative inequality multiplier destroys the bound

For feasible \(f_i(x)\le0\), multiplying by \(\lambda_i<0\) gives \(\lambda_i f_i(x)\ge0\), so the Lagrangian can exceed the primal objective.

Repair. Restrict inequality multipliers to \(\lambda\ge0\).

Nonconvex problems may have a positive gap

The dual sees a convex relaxation of the primal value geometry and may miss nonconvex structure.

Conclusion. KKT conditions may be necessary only locally and need not certify global optimality.

Strong duality can hold without Slater—but is no longer guaranteed

Slater’s condition is sufficient, not necessary. Its failure means the standard theorem cannot be invoked automatically.

Proof discipline. Verify another constraint qualification or prove exactness directly.

The optimal dual value may be approached but not attained

Even when \(d^\star=p^\star\), there need not exist finite multipliers realizing the supremum without additional closedness assumptions.

Distinction. Zero gap and dual attainment are separate properties.
13 · Common mistakes

Precision traps

The most common errors confuse the dual function with the Lagrangian or treat strong duality as automatic.

Incorrect: “The dual function is \(L(x,\lambda,\nu)\).” Correct: \(q(\lambda,\nu)=\inf_xL(x,\lambda,\nu)\).
Incorrect: “The dual problem minimizes \(q\).” Correct: it maximizes the concave dual function over \(\lambda\ge0\).
Incorrect: “Weak duality requires convexity.” Correct: it follows directly from feasibility and multiplier signs.
Incorrect: “Slater is necessary for strong duality.” Correct: it is a standard sufficient condition for convex problems.
Incorrect: “Complementary slackness means every constraint is active.” Correct: each product \(\lambda_i^\star f_i(x^\star)\) is zero; an inactive constraint must have zero multiplier.

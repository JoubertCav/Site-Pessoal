Correspondences, upper hemicontinuity, and selections

A correspondence assigns a set rather than a single point. The right picture is not a curve but a field of vertical fibers: at each state, a whole set of admissible actions or optimal choices may be available. This version rebuilds the graphs so the geometry is explicit: fibers, neighborhoods, good and bad continuity behavior, and a selection traced through a policy correspondence.

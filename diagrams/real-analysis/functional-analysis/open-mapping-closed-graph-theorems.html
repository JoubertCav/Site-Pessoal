Each hypothesis has a precise job

Remove surjectivity or completeness and the conclusions fail in sharply different ways.

Common mistakes

Logical distinctions worth protecting

Tempting claimCorrect statement

A closed graph means the graph is bounded.Closedness is a topological limit property, not a size bound.

Final synthesis

The open mapping theorem converts surjectivity into interior. The bounded inverse theorem converts algebraic invertibility into stable invertibility. The closed graph theorem converts compatibility with limits into a global norm estimate.
